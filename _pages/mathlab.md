Interactive tools for exploring ideas from analysis and signal processing, right in the browser. No installation needed.

## Labs

### [Fourier Series Lab](/mathlab/fourier-series/)

Enter any function, piece by piece if it has jumps, and get its Fourier coefficients (exactly and numerically), the full series and the partial sums S<sub>N</sub>(f). Move the N slider to watch the partial sums converge and the Gibbs phenomenon appear at jumps. Includes trigonometric and complex forms in Stein's notation, a coefficient spectrum and an overshoot chart.
