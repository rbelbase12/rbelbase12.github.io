Exact formulas

Definitions in Stein's notation, then your function worked out symbolically.

Series type

Trigonometric Fourier series

\[ f(x) \sim A_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\Big(A_n\cos\frac{2\pi n x}{L} + B_n\sin\frac{2\pi n x}{L}\Big),\qquad L=b-a \]
\[ A_0=\frac1L\int_a^b f(x)\,dx,\qquad A_n=\frac2L\int_a^b f(x)\cos\frac{2\pi n x}{L}\,dx,\qquad B_n=\frac2L\int_a^b f(x)\sin\frac{2\pi n x}{L}\,dx \]
\[ S_N(f)(x)=A_0+\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Big(A_n\cos\frac{2\pi n x}{L}+B_n\sin\frac{2\pi n x}{L}\Big) \]

Complex Fourier series

\[ \hat f(n)=\frac{1}{L}\int_a^b f(x)\,e^{-2\pi i n x/L}\,dx,\quad n\in\mathbb Z,\qquad f(x)\sim\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\hat f(n)\,e^{2\pi i n x/L},\qquad L=b-a \]
\[ \hat f(0)=A_0,\qquad \hat f(\pm n)=\frac{A_n\mp i\,B_n}{2}\quad(n\ge1,\ f\ \text{real}) \]
\[ S_N(f)(x)=\sum_{|n|\le N}\hat f(n)\,e^{2\pi i n x/L} \]
